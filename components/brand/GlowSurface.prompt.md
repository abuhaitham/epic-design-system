Signature background surface for hero, cover, CTA bands and image placeholders — one per screen.

```jsx
<GlowSurface variant="glow" style={{minHeight:480}}>…</GlowSurface>
<GlowSurface variant="glow-dark">…</GlowSurface>
<GlowSurface variant="fluted" grain={false} style={{aspectRatio:'4/3'}} />
```

Variants: `glow` (blue glow → mist gray, white type), `glow-stone` (glow → Pale Stone, navy type), `glow-dark` (Royal Blue glow on Midnight Navy), `fluted` / `fluted-dark` (vertical ribs), `blue`, `black`, `stone`.
