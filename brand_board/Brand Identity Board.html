01 · B

Why this mark

A bold, abstract geometric mark: solid, architectural and minimalist.

1 2 2 3 3 4 5
Annotated mark
1

Central stem: the institution

A thick vertical rising from the base. The school or institute EPIC helps build, standing upright.

2

Rising arms: growth

Two diagonal arms reach up and outward. Growth, EPIC's third pillar, and expansion to new campuses and leaders.

3

Wide base: the platform

The "Platform" in the company name: the setup and compliance back office everything stands on.

4

Curved junction: continuity

Every edge is sharp except where stem meets base. That single soft curve shows the institution flowing out of its foundation, from licence to launch.

5

Hidden peak: ambition

An angular gap in the lower right forms a mountain peak in the negative space. The summit each client is climbing toward, built into the foundation itself.

Branch and crown

The silhouette reads as a branching symbol and as a stylized crown: organic growth and leadership in one form, fitting a company that develops institutions and their leaders.

Solid and architectural

Thick strokes, sharp edges and a flat base give the mark the weight of a building. Single colour, no effects, matching the brand's calm, premium tone.

Works in both scripts

An abstract symbol, not a letter, so it sits beside "EPIC" and "إبيك" alike. It stays unmirrored in RTL and holds down to a 16px favicon.
